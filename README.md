### Common questions

**My entries aren't showing up on my other device.**
Ad Infinitum syncs through your own iCloud account. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is turned on for Ad Infinitum in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**. Syncing can take a few minutes, especially on the first launch.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
- Check that notifications are allowed in **Settings → Notifications → Ad Infinitum**.
- In the app, go to **Settings → Reminders** and make sure **Send reminders on this device** is on. It's off by default on iPad and Mac, so you don't get every reminder twice.
- Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can delay or silence notifications.

**I get the same reminder on two devices.**
Reminders sync to all your devices. Choose which devices alert you with **Send reminders on this device** in **Settings → Reminders**.

**How do I back up or move my data?**
Your data already syncs with iCloud. For an extra copy, use **Settings → Export or share** to save a backup file, and **Import from file** to restore it.

**How do I delete everything?**
Use **Settings → Erase everything** in the app. See [Deleting your data](#deleting-your-data) to also remove the copy stored in iCloud.

## Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

Ad Infinitum is built for private, personal recovery work. Your entries are yours. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

### The short version

- **I don't collect any of your data.** I have no servers, no accounts, and no way to see anything you write.
- **Your data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account**, on Apple's servers, so it can sync between your devices.
- **No ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics.**

### What the app stores

The app stores the content you create and your settings:

- Your entries, answers, and drafts
- Your sections, questions, groups, and text items (such as prayers and meditations)
- Your glossary terms
- Your reminders and their schedules
- App preferences, such as your theme
- Simple usage statistics shown in the app's **Usage dashboard**, such as how many entries you've written. These are only for you to see.

### Where it's stored

All of this is stored **on your device** and in **your private iCloud database** (Apple's CloudKit), so it stays in sync across the devices signed in to your Apple Account. App preferences sync through iCloud as well.

Data in your private iCloud database is stored by Apple and tied to your Apple Account. It is not accessible to me or to anyone else. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If iCloud is turned off for the app, your data stays only on your device.

### What the app doesn't do

- It doesn't send your data to me or to any server I run.
- It doesn't use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
- It doesn't share or sell your data.
- It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, or any account sign-in.

### Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your device by the app. They aren't sent from any server. You can make any reminder **discreet**, so the lock screen shows only "You have a reminder" without the section, entry type, or message.

### Exporting and sharing

If you use **Export or share**, the app creates a file containing the data you chose and saves or shares it wherever you pick. After that, the file is under your control. The app never sends it anywhere on its own.

### Deleting your data

- **In the app:** **Settings → Erase everything** deletes your data from the device and, through sync, from your iCloud database.
- **From iCloud directly:** on iPhone or iPad, go to **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**, choose Ad Infinitum, and delete its data.
- Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

### Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, because it doesn't collect information at all.
